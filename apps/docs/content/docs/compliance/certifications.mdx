## 21 CFR Part 11

<Callout type="info">Status: Compliant (Enterprise License)</Callout>

21 CFR Part 11 is a regulation by the FDA that establishes the criteria for electronic records and electronic signatures to ensure their authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality in the pharmaceutical, medical device, and other FDA-regulated industries.

Read more about [21 CFR Part 11 with Hanzo Sign](https://esign.hanzo.ai/21-CFR-Part-11).

### Main Requirements

- Strong Identity Checks for each Signature
- Signature and Audit Trails
- User Access Management
- Quality Assurance Documentation

## SOC 2

<Callout type="info">Status: [Compliant with the SOC 2 Type II control set](https://esign.hanzo.ai/trust)</Callout>

SOC 2 is a framework for managing and auditing the security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and data privacy in cloud and IT service organizations, established by the AICPA.

Controls aligned to the SOC 2 Type II control set — encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, and audit logging.

Hanzo Sign is compliant with the SOC 2 Type II control set, and ISO 27001 and HIPAA controls are aligned — including a BAA where one is needed. Tell us what your procurement needs and we will map it to the controls.

## ISO 27001

<Callout type="warn">Status: [Planned](https://github.com/hanzo-sign/backlog/issues/26)</Callout>

ISO 27001 is an international standard for managing information security, specifying requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS).

## HIPAA

<Callout type="warn">Status: [Planned](https://github.com/hanzo-sign/backlog/issues/25)</Callout>

The HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) is a U.S. law designed to protect patient health information's privacy and security and improve the healthcare system's efficiency and effectiveness.
